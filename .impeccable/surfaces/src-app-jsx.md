# Surface brief: 8 Ball Studio Story (src/App.jsx)

Scope: Studio, Projects, and Contact Pages, the intro overlay, and all chrome. The 3D pool-break Intro scenes are kept unchanged.
Mode: Persuade. Audience: KL SMB/F&B owners and regional marketing teams, weighted equally. Success: a message on WhatsApp, Instagram, or email.
Proof: 4 client logos only; never invent case studies or metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the studio floor. After the break in the dark hall, the lights come up on an infinity cove, and each Page is the same cyc wall relit with a new gel. It refuses the category default: a near-black page with one neon accent and a card marquee.

OWN-WORLD:
- Cyc-white paint relit per Page by saturated gels: Bright Pink #ef3f86 (Studio), Teal #16a597 (Projects), Amber #f59e1b (Contact). Each wall has a physically motivated hotspot and a cove shading line.
- Gaffer-black (#17171a) silhouette type in Archivo: extra-condensed 900 for titles, expanded caps for labels.
- Paper-tape labels for nav, services, and the draft switcher. Spike-tape marks as the page indicator. A taped call sheet for contact.

STORY: The visitor sees the break (craft). Then the lights come up on 8 BALL STUDIO, with the three services taped on the floor. Next, the client roster glides along the floor under a teal key. Last is a call sheet whose three rows are the only thing left to do.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Studio, the first view after the Intro):
- The pink-gel wall fills the frame.
- 8 BALL / STUDIO in gaffer-black silhouette, two lines, about 16vw, standing on the cove line at about 66% height. It has a soft floor contact shadow and a wall shadow cast from the key light (the pointer).
- Three service tape labels sit on the floor below; the location label is bottom-left; the header tapes are top-right.
- Primary action: the Contact Us tape, plus the spike marks at right.

FORM: Cyc Wall, #1 on the re-roll grounded list; seed key 9f472c35 (re-roll 1).
- Signature interaction: the lighting cue. Each Page arrives as a gel flood from its own light position (clip-path circle), and the type appears as a silhouette as the wall lights. The pointer is the key light: it moves the hotspot and the cast shadow.
- Motion: one CustomEase "cue" (fast rise, long tail), scrubbed inside the master timeline.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
