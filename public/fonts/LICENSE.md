# Archivo

`archivo-latin-var.woff2` is the Latin subset of Archivo (variable: width 62–125%, weight 100–900),
downloaded from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com, Archivo v25) and self-hosted so it can be preloaded.

Designed by Omnibus-Type. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org

# Look faces

Latin subsets downloaded from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) and self-hosted. Each is licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org

- `anybody-latin-var.woff2`: Anybody (width 50–150%, weight 100–900), by Tyler Finck / Etcetera Type Co. Used by the Baize look.
- `saira-latin-var.woff2`: Saira (width 50–125%, weight 100–900), by Omnibus-Type. Used by the Crucible look.
- `big-shoulders-display-latin-var.woff2`: Big Shoulders Display (weight 100–900), by Patric King / XXIX. Used by the Safelight look.
- `jost-latin-var.woff2`: Jost (weight 100–900), by Owen Earl / indestructible type*. Used by the House Print look.
- `antonio-latin-var.woff2`: Antonio (weight 100–700), by Vernon Adams. Used by the 16mm look.
- `martian-mono-latin-var.woff2`: Martian Mono (width 75–112.5%, weight 100–800), by Evil Martians. Used by the 16mm look's edge codes.
- `league-gothic-latin-var.woff2`: League Gothic (width 75–100%), by The League of Moveable Type. Used by the One-Sheet look.
- `oswald-latin-var.woff2`: Oswald (weight 200–700), by Vernon Adams. Used by the Table Board look.
- `anton-latin.woff2`: Anton (400), by Vernon Adams. Used by the Wild Posting look.
- `sofia-sans-extra-condensed-latin-var.woff2`: Sofia Sans Extra Condensed (weight 1–1000), by Lettersoup / Botio Nikoltchev. Used by the Marker Board look's chalk.
- `marcellus-sc-latin.woff2`: Marcellus SC (400), by Astigmatic. Used by the Marker Board look's engraved brass.
- `schibsted-grotesk-latin-var.woff2`: Schibsted Grotesk (weight 400–900), by Bakken & Bæck. Used by the Downlight look.

Schibsted Grotesk (Downlight, the default look) and Archivo (shared body copy) are preloaded; the others load when their look first renders text.
