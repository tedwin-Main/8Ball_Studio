# Wood surface maps

Source: [Wood Table 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001), Poly Haven.
Photography: Dimitrios Savva. Processing: Rico Cilliers.
License: [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

The three original 2K JPEG maps are re-encoded as WebP and bundled locally as
`walnut-color.webp`, `walnut-normal.webp` (OpenGL tangent-space), and
`walnut-roughness.webp`. The low quality tier downsamples them to 1K at runtime.
The material tints the original stained wood to a smoked walnut finish.
Felt, leather, and resin microsurface maps are generated by project code.
